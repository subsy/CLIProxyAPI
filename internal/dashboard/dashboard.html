    How to read this

    Draw-down order is the order the current strategy tries accounts in. With reset-soonest, the account whose weekly window resets first is drained first, because its remaining quota is lost at reset. Accounts not seen since start-up get one request so their reset time is learned. Accounts whose 5-hour window is nearly full are moved to the back. With round-robin, all usable accounts share traffic evenly.

    A green dot marks an account that served a request in the last three minutes.

    Expires unused is the share of a weekly window that would be lost at reset if usage continues at the current pace. The thin marker on a meter shows where usage is heading by reset.

    Prompt cache hit is the share of cached prompt tokens read from the cache rather than written. Each time a session switches accounts, its cache is rebuilt on the new account.
